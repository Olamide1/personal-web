VI

Rebook

Win back lapsed clients. Grow your reviews.

Rebook is client reactivation and review generation for salons and other repeat-visit businesses. Import your client list once from a CSV (Vagaro, Fresha, Square, GlossGenius or any spreadsheet) and it takes over: it flags clients who have gone quiet past a threshold you set, sends a three-email win-back sequence in the salon's own name that stops the moment they rebook, reply or unsubscribe, and sends a Google review request after each visit. A dashboard tracks rebookings, reviews collected and recovered revenue. Currently paused while we pivot.
