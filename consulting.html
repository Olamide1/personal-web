      Five days, one finished piece each day

      A fresco painter finishes one patch of wet plaster a day, a giornata, and never leaves it half done. The teardown works the same way. Each rosette below gains a band, as the work does.

      1. I

        Day 1: Data in

        You send step-level funnel events and your verification vendor's result codes. No personal data and no customer documents.

      2. II

        Day 2: The map

        I measure completion and first-attempt success at every step, by device, country and document type.

      3. III

        Day 3: The causes

        I trace the biggest losses to delivery failures, capture problems, rejection reasons, unclear copy, support tickets and public app reviews.

      4. IV

        Day 4: What each fix is worth

        Every fix gets an estimate in verified customers and in acquisition cost recovered.

      5. V

        Day 5: The readout

        A 60-minute walkthrough and a 30-day fix plan your team can run.

      VVVV WHAT YOU KEEP
      • A funnel map. Every step measured by device, country and document type, so you see exactly where applicants leave.
      • Ranked causes. The three to five fixable reasons behind the biggest losses, each backed by your own data.
      • Every fix sized in outcomes. Verified customers gained and acquisition cost recovered, so your team knows what to build first.
      • A 30-day plan. Run by your team, walked through with you in a 60-minute readout.

      Fixed fee: €3,600 to €5,000, depending on the number of flows and markets. Three fixable causes, or you don't pay.

      A cause counts when your own data backs it and I can put an estimated impact on it. The five days start when usable data arrives, and if it never does, we stop and you owe nothing.

      Fintechs, wallets, lenders, crypto on-ramps and insurtechs with a live onboarding flow and a verification vendor such as Veriff, Sumsub, Onfido or Persona.

      What I need from you

      • Aggregated funnel events by step, with device and country.
      • Your vendor's rejection and retry codes.
      • A 30-minute call at the start and the readout at the end.

      I sign an NDA first. I receive only aggregated counts by step, device and country, plus vendor result codes. No names, documents, images or customer-level records. Files are kept encrypted on one machine, used only for your teardown, and deleted within 30 days of the readout, with written confirmation.
